About

I am a biomedical engineer and research assistant in the Cell and Tissue Engineering Lab, Department of Biomedical Engineering, at the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST). I earned my B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering from MIST, with the degree conferred by Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP).

My research centres on natural-extract-based hydrogels for wound care: formulating PVA- and gelatin-based hydrogels loaded with garlic, Nigella sativa, flaxseed and zinc oxide, and measuring their mechanical, physical and antibacterial behaviour. My undergraduate thesis on a garlic-extract hydrogel won a Best Paper Presentation Award at WCASET-23 in Malaysia. Alongside the lab work, I trained in infectious-disease laboratory techniques and molecular biology at the Child Health Research Foundation (CHRF), including nucleic-acid extraction, primer design, PCR and qRT-PCR. I want to pursue graduate research that connects biomaterials with molecular and cellular biology to address infection and tissue repair, particularly in low-resource health settings.

Nigella sativa Extract-Based Hydrogels May 2023 – Aug 2024

Evaluated the mechanical strength, chemical composition and antibacterial activity of hydrogels made with Nigella sativa extract. Higher extract concentrations increased tensile strength and rigidity. Pure thymoquinone needed high concentrations to inhibit bacteria, but was more effective in combination with other antibacterial compounds.

Garlic-Extract Incorporated PVA/Gelatin Hydrogel Apr 2022 – Feb 2023 · Undergraduate thesis

Formulated PVA/gelatin hydrogels incorporating garlic extract and assessed them by disc diffusion, swelling and moisture-retention tests. The garlic-juice hydrogel showed the highest antibacterial activity and withstood friction comparable to human skin, and its swelling and moisture retention make it a strong candidate for wound dressings. Presented at WCASET-23 (Best Paper Presentation Award).
